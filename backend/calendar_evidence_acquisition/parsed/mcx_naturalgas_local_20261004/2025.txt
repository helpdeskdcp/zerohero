                     Due date rate shall be the settlement price, in Indian rupees, of the New
                     York Mercantile Exchange’s (NYMEX)# Natural Gas (NG) front month
                     contract on the last trading day of the MCX Natural Gas contract. The
                     last available RBI USDINR reference rate will be used for the conversion.
                     The price so arrived will be rounded off to the nearest tick.
