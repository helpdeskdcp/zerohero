                          Due date rate shall be the settlement price, in Indian rupees, of the

                          front month contract on the last trading day of the MCX Natural
                          Gas contract. The last available RBI USDINR reference rate will be

 Due Date Rate (Final
                          For example, on the day of expiry, if NYMEX Natural Gas (NG)
