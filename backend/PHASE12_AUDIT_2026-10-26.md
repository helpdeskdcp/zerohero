## 7. Frontend / transport (VERIFIED)
- **nginx `gzip on` compresses only text/html** (default `gzip_types`): `arena.js` is served at 24,057 B uncompressed. With JS/CSS/JSON types enabled: `app.js` 175,847 → 47,209 B, `lightweight-charts` 197,922 → 62,150 B, `smartchart-vendor.js` 3,310,986 → 887,049 B (gzip -9). **BLOCKED**: it is the VPS-wide nginx.conf shared with other apps — needs the owner’s approval (`gzip_types application/javascript text/css application/json; gzip_vary on;` then `nginx -t && reload`).
- 4 independent `esc()` helpers and 8 copies of the `:root` colour tokens in inline `<style>`: real but tiny (≈ 1 KB each); a shared `theme.css` + `util.js` is the clean fix, low value, deferred.
- Smart Chart’s 3.3 MB vendor bundle is already on-demand (only smartchart.html). `app.js` (176 KB, unminified, single file for the dashboard) is the one worthwhile splitting target; needs a build step the repo does not have → NOT VERIFIED benefit, deferred.
