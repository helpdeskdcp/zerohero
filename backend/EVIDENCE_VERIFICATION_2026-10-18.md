## 3. No future data / leakage (executed: `scripts/verify_no_leakage.py`)
Prefix-invariance at 3 mid-session cut points (2020-06-15 12:30, 2023-03-09 14:05, 2025-06-18 10:45): the corrected engine run on truncated data reproduces every trade complete before the cut record-for-record (550, 1,062, 1,459 trades; 0 differences); positions still open at the data end are force-closed with reason EOD_DATA (excluded; their entries exist unchanged in the full run - the first version of this check flagged exactly one such trade, investigated, and it was this boundary artefact); patterns, zones and bracket outcomes are identical on 55,875 / 107,162 / 149,132 earlier bars and 400 events per cut. Structure: signals use bar i and earlier only, entries at the next open, ATR is Wilder-smoothed (causal), previous-day levels use finished days, option data at entry/exit times only, the option strike window was re-centred on the previous close (the earlier expiry-day-close look-ahead in the straddle scan was found and fixed). Not covered by any of this: parameter choice that was informed by earlier looks at 2024+ (documented).

## 4. Conclusion on the question
The evidence shows that **the specific tested hypotheses failed**, not that the family is exhausted. Adequately tested: candlestick-pattern/zone entries and the Hybrid MP entry rules on NIFTY 5m OHLC at costs of 2-6.3 bps (power ~2.4 bps; 0 of 144 even weakly positive). NOT tested: other timeframes and instruments at scale, tick/quote-level signals, event/flow/OI information, option-structure strategies with bid/ask data, adaptive/regime models, forward (live-paper) evidence. Reasonable decision: abandon these tested rule definitions on 5m OHLC; keep the family open only for hypotheses with a new information source or a cost structure that can accommodate a signal.
