### Step 2 (futures + chain wired) — limits that remain
- Freshness is cadence-aware ONLY for stored-history modes (measured capture cadence: futures ~160 s, greek snapshots ~960 s -> limits 420 s / 1800 s, env `NIFTY_DIR_CAPTURED_FUT_MAX_AGE_SEC` / `..._CHAIN_MAX_AGE_SEC`); the live limit stays 60 s. A future live mode fed from the same capture would exceed 60 s by construction (the capture is slower than that) and needs either a faster feed or a deliberate, documented limit.
- OI/PCR is near-money only (ATM +/-10 strikes): labelled `oi_scope: NEAR_MONEY` end to end, never presented as full-chain PCR. Greeks/IV cover the whole chain.
- The instrument master is the CURRENT master: a futures/option expiry that has already expired is not in it, so identity is reported as CAPTURED_NOT_IN_CURRENT_MASTER (captured token + expiry as evidence) instead of LISTED_IN_MASTER. A token that contradicts a listed contract is refused.
- Tests: tests/test_nifty_direction_derivatives.py (10): point-in-time causality (later rows, rows received after as_of, bad broker status ignored; later data does not change an earlier answer), missing fields/token mismatch -> unavailable, expired contract skipped, chain band/OI/oldest-timestamp, no greeks / no spot / failed status -> no chain, snapshots after as_of ignored, cadence-aware limits, adapter capabilities. 80 targeted tests pass.
