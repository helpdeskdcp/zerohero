## What this does and does not say
- It says the mechanical Pine signal, taken at its closing bar with conservative fills, did not make money over 11 years of NIFTY index data nor over four months of actual weekly option premiums. The owner's "4 profitable trades" are n=4 and may reflect discretion (which signals to take, when to exit) that the script does not contain; that is not testable here.
- Limits: one 4-month regime for options; no bid/ask in the data (slippage is an assumed stand-in); 30 5m / 12 15m signals fell outside the downloaded strikes and 479 API fetches failed during the pull (coverage loss is reported, not filled); position_open rejects most 5m signals (one position at a time); the THIRDS split is analytical (a 1-lot position cannot be split in thirds); TradingView parity is BLOCKED (no TradingView export), so port-vs-original differences in tie rules/early-bar behaviour are unverified.
- Not claimed: profitability, production readiness, or that the script has no edge when used with discretion.
