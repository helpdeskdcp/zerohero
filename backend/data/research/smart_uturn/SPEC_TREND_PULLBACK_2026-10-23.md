# Chanakya Smart Trend-Pullback v1 — mirror replay spec, pre-registered 2026-10-23
The owner asked for a new TradingView indicator ("smart strategy"). Its logic was fixed in app/smart_uturn/pine/chanakya_smart_trend_pullback_v1.pine BEFORE any replay and is NOT tuned afterwards. A Python mirror (app/smart_uturn/trend_pullback.py) reproduces the Pine logic bar by bar so the claim on the dashboard can be checked on 11 years of NIFTY 1-minute data.
Rule (Pine defaults): HTF trend = EMA21 vs EMA50 on the HTF plus RSI vs 50 (previous completed HTF bar); local EMA21 vs EMA50; pullback = low (high) touched EMA21 within 6 bars (tolerance 0.15 ATR); trigger = close back beyond EMA21, close beyond the previous bar's high (low), RSI above 45 (below 55) and turning; min ATR 2 bps; window 09:25-15:00 IST; cooldown 5; max 4 signals/day; stop signalling after 2 losing trades; stop = 8-bar swing extreme +/- 0.2 ATR (min 0.5 ATR, skipped if wider than 2.5 ATR); targets 1R/2R/3R with thirds, stop first, flat at 15:15; one trade at a time; entry = signal-bar close.
Charts and HTFs (fixed): 5m with HTF 15m and 60m; 15m with HTF 60m and 240m. Data: Kaggle NIFTY 1-minute, P1 2015-2019, P2 2020-2025. Costs: 2.0 index points per round trip (an assumption), reported both gross and net.
Verdict rule: EDGE only if for BOTH charts and BOTH periods n >= 30 and the 95% bootstrap interval (2000 resamples, seed 7) of NET mean R per trade has a lower bound above 0. Else NO EDGE. Descriptive: win rate, T1/T2/T3 reach rates, sum of R.
Not claimed: any edge. Entry at the signal close is optimistic (a real fill is the next open); this replay mirrors the Pine scoreboard, so it is an upper bound on what the dashboard shows.
