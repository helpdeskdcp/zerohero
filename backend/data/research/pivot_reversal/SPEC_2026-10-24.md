# Pivot-line reversal: engulfing / harami near a pivot line after consolidation — pre-registered spec (2026-10-24, before any result)

## The rule (owner's words: "near the pivot lines, if a bullish engulfing or harami candle forms, give a buy or sell signal after consolidation")
Bar t (5-minute, closed) fires when ALL hold:
1. Pattern (TA-Lib canonical CDLENGULFING / CDLHARAMI, +100 = bullish -> BUY, -100 = bearish -> SELL) at bar t.
2. Pivot proximity: the pattern bar's LOW (BUY) / HIGH (SELL) lies within 0.30 x ATR14 of one of the 13 pivot lines computed from the PREVIOUS session's H, L, C: classical P, R1 = 2P-L, S1 = 2P-H, R2 = P+(H-L), S2 = P-(H-L), R3 = H+2(P-L), S3 = L-2(H-P), and Fibonacci R1/R2/R3 = P + 0.382/0.618/1.000 x (H-L), S1/S2/S3 = P - same.
3. Consolidation: the 10 bars before bar t (t-10 .. t-1), all in the same session (so t is at least the 11th bar), span (max high - min low) <= 2.5 x ATR14 at t-1.
Signal = BUY or SELL at the close of bar t; entry = open of bar t+1; exit = close of bar t+h, h in {3, 6, 12} bars (15 / 30 / 60 minutes), same session only. A signal within h bars of the last counted one of the same variant is skipped.
Variants (K = 4): ENGULF_BUY, ENGULF_SELL, HARAMI_BUY, HARAMI_SELL.

## Data, periods, cost
NIFTY and BANKNIFTY 5-minute index bars 2015-2026 (Kaggle research_historical.db, de-duplicated). P1 = 2015-2020, P2 = 2021-2026. Round-trip cost 1.0 bp (index-futures proxy; 0.5 / 2.0 bp reported, not gated). Return in bps, side-adjusted, after cost.

## Gates for GO-SHADOW (per variant; ALL)
For NIFTY AND BANKNIFTY, in P1 AND P2, at >= 2 of the 3 horizons: n >= 300 counted signals per cell, mean net return > 0, and day-block bootstrap CI lower bound > 0 at the Bonferroni level 0.05 / 8 (4 variants x 2 instruments). Fewer than 300 signals in any cell -> INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE for that variant; a variant with enough data failing any gate -> NO-GO.
Descriptive baselines (not gated), same horizons: pattern + proximity WITHOUT consolidation; pattern + consolidation WITHOUT proximity; pattern alone; and the unconditional same-side drift. They show what each of the owner's two filters contributes.
