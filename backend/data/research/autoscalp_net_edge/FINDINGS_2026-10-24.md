Findings
1. The stored r_multiple (mean +0.17) is NOT a rupee result: it is a different, un-costed ratio (64 of 178 rows differ from (exit-entry)/(entry-SL) by more than 0.05; NIFTY 57.5 -> 69.3 with SL 44.1 is stored as +4.40R but is +0.88R on the premium stop distance). It made the engine look profitable. Real rupee expectancy after costs is clearly negative overall.
2. The gross signal edge is about zero (-Rs 80 per lot-trade, CI includes 0 for BANKNIFTY / SENSEX); friction (Rs 185) is larger than |gross| in 28 % of trades and larger than the typical gain.
3. A friction-based entry gate does not rescue it: trades with friction <= 15 % / 25 % / 40 % of the stop distance still lose Rs -295 / -214 / -209 per trade (the low-friction trades are index options whose gross edge is negative too). So the lever is not "skip expensive trades"; it is that the signal itself has no demonstrated gross edge.
4. NATURALGAS / CRUDEOIL options: friction is 60 % / 37 % of the median stop distance (risk Rs 387 / 780 per lot vs friction Rs 244 / 268).

What was built (observation + opt-in, nothing changes by default)
- GET /api/autoscalp/net-edge: the table above, live.
- Safeguards.net_edge_enforce (default OFF): when ON, a symbol marked SUSPEND_RECOMMENDED (>= 40 trades and 90 % upper bound < 0) gets no new paper entries. Left OFF on purpose: paper trades are free evidence and the arena's AI participant is fed by autoscalp.
- LIVE entry gate (4th gate, fails closed): AngelOneBroker refuses an ENTRY order unless the underlying is EVIDENCE_OK (last 100 resolved trades with a positive 90 % lower bound). Exits, stops and cancels are never blocked. Explicit owner bypass: CHANAKYA_NET_EDGE_OVERRIDE=1. LIVE is already off (execution_mode, CHANAKYA_ALLOW_LIVE, confirm token), so this changes nothing today; it prevents arming LIVE on a symbol with no measured edge.
Thresholds (40 trades / 100 trades / 90 % CI) were fixed on economic grounds after the first look at this data: not tuned on it, but not pre-registered either.
