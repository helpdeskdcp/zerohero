# H-GAP1 — opening gap: fade or continuation? — pre-registered spec, 2026-10-23

Written BEFORE any result is computed. Frozen once the first result is printed; changes go in a dated addendum. Price-only study (no options, no costs).

## Question
After a day opens with a gap versus the previous close, does the index on average continue in the gap direction or fade back, measured from 09:30 to the close, large enough to matter?
Already covered elsewhere (not repeated): expiry-day premise and expiry-day straddles (NO-GO), index-event option-level (NO-GO), level breakout/reversal (H-LT2, no separation).

## Data / periods
NIFTY 1m 2015-2025 and BANKNIFTY 1m 2015-2026-04 (same files and loader as H-LT1). P1 = 2015-2019, P2 = 2020+. Both instruments, both periods = 4 cells; every cell must agree.

## Definitions (fixed now)
- gap g = open of the 09:15 bar / previous session close (15:29 bar close) - 1. Sessions with fewer than 340 bars are skipped.
- sigma_d = standard deviation of the previous 20 sessions' close-to-close returns (needs 20 prior sessions). z = g / sigma_d.
- Gap day: |z| >= 0.5. Buckets: A = 0.5..1, B = 1..2, C >= 2; direction UP/DOWN by sign of g.
- Entry reference = close of the 09:29 bar (information known at 09:30), exit = close of the 15:14 bar. R = exit/entry - 1 (in basis points).
- Signed return: CONT = sign(g) * R (positive = continuation), FADE = -CONT.

## Metrics
Per cell and per bucket: n days, mean CONT in bps, 95% CI by bootstrap over days (2000 resamples, seed 7), hit rate of CONT > 0. Descriptive only: share of gap days where price trades back to the previous close before 15:15 (gap fill).

## Pass / fail (decided now)
- CONTINUATION EDGE (or FADE EDGE): the all-gap-days mean CONT has the same sign in all 4 cells, its CI excludes 0 in every cell, AND |mean| >= 8 bps in every cell. 8 bps is a rough index-level hurdle: earlier work found option theta/spread/costs erase smaller index-level moves.
- Otherwise NO GAP EDGE. Bucket results are descriptive and cannot rescue a failed all-days test (no forking by bucket after the fact).
- A pass would still need an option-premium spec (premium, spread, costs, stop/target) before any trade rule.

## Not claimed
No edge, no profitability. NG/Crude/GOLDM/SENSEX not covered (no comparable long 1-minute history).
