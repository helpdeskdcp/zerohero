# H-LT1 level-touch — findings (2026-10-23)
Spec: SPEC_2026-10-23.md (committed before any result). Code: run_study.py, addendum_recal.py. Data: NIFTY 1m 2015-2025, BANKNIFTY 1m 2015-2026-04; P1 = 2015-2019, P2 = 2020+.

## 1. Pre-registered test: the driftless barrier model is MISCALIBRATED (all 4 cells)
p_hat = 2(1-Phi(d)), sigma10 from EWMA of 1-minute returns. Reliability error 0.034-0.045 (limit 0.03), slope 0.81-0.95.
Direction is the same in both periods and both instruments: it OVER-predicts middle/high touch probabilities by 5-8 points (p_hat 0.80 -> observed 0.73-0.76; 0.62 -> 0.55-0.57) and UNDER-predicts the far tail (0.045 -> 0.05-0.078). Brier skill vs the base rate 0.21-0.26.

After the map: reliability error 0.010-0.013, slope 0.93-1.09 in all 4 cells (limit 0.03 / [0.8,1.2]). Stable across 2015-19 and 2020-26 and across both indices.

## 3. Do structural levels (swing H/L, previous-day H/L, opening-range H/L) matter? (addendum, not gating)
The first lift in results.json compares against the biased model, so it is confounded (-3 points everywhere is mostly the model's bias). The corrected comparison (observed plain-level rate at the same distance, addendum_results.json):
structural levels are touched slightly LESS often than a plain price at the same distance: NIFTY -1.3/-1.6 points (previous-day H/L -2.4..-3.8), BANKNIFTY -0.2/-0.6 points. Same sign in both periods, tiny, and no bootstrap CI was computed for the corrected comparison, so the spec's bar for "level information" (CI excludes 0 in both periods and both indices) is NOT met. Provisional verdict: NO MEASURED LEVEL INFORMATION beyond distance; if anything previous-day H/L act as mild barriers on NIFTY.

## What this is and is not
Usable: a calibrated touch-probability table for the next 10 minutes as a risk/targets/veto input ("a target 1 sigma10 away is touched ~28% of the time").
Not shown: any direction forecast, edge, or P&L. Option premium, spread and costs are not modelled. Reversal-vs-breakout after a touch is a separate spec.
