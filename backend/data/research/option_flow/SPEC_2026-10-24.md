# Option-market flow as NEW information for the NIFTY index — pre-registered spec (2026-10-24, before any option data is pulled or any feature computed)

## Why
Every price-only rule tested so far (patterns, pivots, ranges, indicators, GARCH/HMM, scans) has no net edge: they use information the market has already priced. Option volume / open-interest flow and put-call parity are information of a different kind. Question: does the NIFTY weekly-option flow at minute t predict the NIFTY index move over the next 30 minutes, beyond what price-only features already say?

## Data (read-only, Upstox Expired-Instruments + historical candles; new store data/historical/option_flow/option_flow.db)
NIFTY weekly expiries from 2024-10-03 to 2026-10-20. For each expiry the last 5 trading sessions up to expiry. Contracts: strikes within +/-300 points (13 strikes, step 50) of the index open at the start of each of those sessions, calls and puts, union over the 5 days. Per contract 1-minute bars: close, volume, open interest. Index: NIFTY 1-minute (market_history.db market_candles INDEX). Nearest not-yet-expired weekly only.

## Features at the close of every 5-minute index bar t (session bars only, t at least the 7th bar), using only data <= t
ATM(t) = the strike (step 50) nearest to the index close at t; "near strikes" = ATM +/- 250 that were pulled.
- F1 put/call volume imbalance over the last 15 minutes: (PutVol - CallVol)/(PutVol + CallVol) over near strikes.
- F2 OI-change imbalance over the last 15 minutes: (dPutOI - dCallOI)/(|dPutOI| + |dCallOI|), d = OI(t) - OI(t-15 min), near strikes.
- F3 synthetic-future basis change: D(t) = (K + C_ATM(t) - P_ATM(t)) - S(t); F3 = (D(t) - D(t-15 min)) / S(t) in bp.
- F4 straddle (implied-vol) change: ST(t)/ST(t-15 min) - 1, ST = C_ATM + P_ATM.
- F5 skew change: ((P_{ATM-100} - C_{ATM+100})/ST)(t) - same(t-15 min).
Price-only baseline B: index returns over the last 5 / 15 / 30 minutes divided by ATR(14) of 5-minute bars, and a time-of-day bucket.

## Target
y = 1 if the NIFTY index close at t+30 minutes (same session) is above the close at t; ret = that move in bp; entry at the next minute's open is approximated by close(t) (the 30-minute edge is judged after a 1.0 bp round-trip cost).

## Models and walk-forward
M_B = logistic regression on baseline B. M_F = logistic regression on B + F1..F5 (features standardised with train statistics). M_G = HistGradientBoosting on B + F1..F5. Quarterly walk-forward: train on all earlier quarters (at least 2), test the next quarter; test quarters 2025Q1 .. 2026Q3 (about 7). 1-day embargo.

## Gates for GO-SHADOW (ALL; K = 2 model families -> CI level 0.025 two-sided)
G1 pooled test AUC of M_F (or M_G) >= 0.53 with day-block bootstrap CI lower bound > 0.50.
G2 AUC lift over M_B >= 0.010 with paired day-block bootstrap CI lower bound > 0.
G3 signals = test bars beyond the train 90th percentile of |p - 0.5|: n >= 300, mean directional 30-minute return minus 1.0 bp > 0, day-block bootstrap CI lower bound > 0.
G4 AUC > 0.50 in at least 5 of the test quarters.
Else NO-GO (INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE if fewer than 300 signals or fewer than 5 test quarters). Univariate AUC of each of F1..F5 is reported, not gated.

## Not claimed
Index-level, 30-minute horizon, assumed 1 bp cost; no option fills. A pass would license an observation-only live feed. The weekly-expiry day (0DTE) is included; it is reported separately as a descriptive split.
