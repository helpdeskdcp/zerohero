Reading
- It is the first thing in this project that adds a measurable, direction-consistent lift over price-only features (+0.010 to +0.012 AUC, CI lower bound within 0.001 of zero) and whose top-decile signals reach break-even (gross about 1 bp vs a 1 bp cost) rather than losing 1-2 bp. It is still not a pass: AUC 0.52 < 0.53, the lift CI touches zero, the net CI includes zero, and the signal is a gross 1 bp on a 30-minute horizon.
- The price-only baseline on this 2025-26 window is only 0.508, much weaker than the 0.52-0.53 of 2018-2024 (the older price-only mean-reversion decayed), so the option flow lift is on top of a baseline that is almost nothing.
- What carries it is put-call parity: the 15-minute change of (K + C - P) - S, i.e. the options market's implied forward moving against the cash index. Volume and OI imbalance add almost nothing.
Not claimed: no option fills, spread or 0DTE gamma effects; one underlying (NIFTY), one horizon (30 min), 8 test quarters.
Candidate follow-ups (each needs its own pre-registered spec): replicate on BANKNIFTY / SENSEX; use all sessions of each expiry (more samples) and finer parity features (bid/ask, not 1-minute closes); 15- and 60-minute horizons.
