# Option-market flow as new information for NIFTY — findings 2026-10-24
Spec (committed e2cfb38 before any data was pulled): SPEC_2026-10-24.md. Code: app/candle_ai/option_flow.py (causality + sign tests in tests/test_option_flow.py), scripts/option_flow_pull.py, scripts/option_flow_study.py, results.json (run 1 kept as results_run1.json: only a descriptive 0DTE flag was fixed afterwards, all gated numbers identical).
Data: NIFTY weekly options, 108 expiries 2024-10-03 .. 2026-10-19, last 5 sessions of each, ATM +/-300 points, 1-minute close / volume / open interest (8.68M rows from the Upstox Expired-Instruments API, now stored in data/historical/option_flow/option_flow.db, 709 MB); index 1-minute from market_candles. 32,181 five-minute decision points, 28,350 in the 8 walk-forward test quarters (2025Q1 .. 2026Q4). Target: NIFTY 30-minute direction, cost 1 bp.

Verdict (pre-registered gates): NO-GO — but the closest result of anything tested in this project.

Univariate AUC: F3 synthetic-future basis change 0.518 (the carrier of the signal), F5 skew change 0.507, F4 straddle change 0.506, F2 OI-change imbalance 0.505, F1 put/call volume imbalance 0.495 (slightly inverse). AUC is above 0.50 in 7 of 8 test quarters (2026Q1 0.494 is the exception); expiry day 0.512-0.518 vs other days 0.519 (no 0DTE artefact).

Reading
- It is the first thing in this project that adds a measurable, direction-consistent lift over price-only features (+0.010 to +0.012 AUC, CI lower bound within 0.001 of zero) and whose top-decile signals reach break-even (gross about 1 bp vs a 1 bp cost) rather than losing 1-2 bp. It is still not a pass: AUC 0.52 < 0.53, the lift CI touches zero, the net CI includes zero, and the signal is a gross 1 bp on a 30-minute horizon.
- The price-only baseline on this 2025-26 window is only 0.508, much weaker than the 0.52-0.53 of 2018-2024 (the older price-only mean-reversion decayed), so the option flow lift is on top of a baseline that is almost nothing.
- What carries it is put-call parity: the 15-minute change of (K + C - P) - S, i.e. the options market's implied forward moving against the cash index. Volume and OI imbalance add almost nothing.
Not claimed: no option fills, spread or 0DTE gamma effects; one underlying (NIFTY), one horizon (30 min), 8 test quarters.
Candidate follow-ups (each needs its own pre-registered spec): replicate on BANKNIFTY / SENSEX; use all sessions of each expiry (more samples) and finer parity features (bid/ask, not 1-minute closes); 15- and 60-minute horizons.

Pre-registered replication criterion: (a) lift >= +0.005 for both models: yes (+0.0112, +0.0110); (b) F3 univariate AUC > 0.510: yes (0.517); (c) AUC > 0.50 in >= 5 test quarters: yes -> REPLICATES. STRONG replication (original G1-G4 on SENSEX): no (AUC < 0.53, net after cost <= 0).
Reading
- Two independent index markets give the same picture: price-only features are nearly useless on 2025-26 data (0.504-0.508), and adding the options market's information lifts the AUC by about +0.011 in both, carried by the same feature (the 15-minute change of the put-call-parity synthetic future minus the index, F3 ~0.517-0.518 in both). That is the first result in this project that survives an independent replication.
- It is a real but small information effect: the best 10 % of signals earn about +0.5 to +1.2 bp gross over 30 minutes, which is break-even to slightly negative after the cost of trading the index future (1.0-1.5 bp). Not tradable as a standalone rule.
- Per the pre-registered consequences: the option-flow line is worth a NEW pre-registered extension (all sessions of each expiry, bid/ask-level parity instead of 1-minute closes, 15 / 60-minute horizons, use as a confirmation filter for other signals) and an observation-only live feed. Not an order gate.
