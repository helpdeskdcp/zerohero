# Pre-registered spec: 10-day replay of the AI side under the Institution-arena rules (written BEFORE any outcome was computed)

**Question.** If the arena's settlement rules had been applied to the AI's own recorded AUTOSCALP paper signals of the last 10 days, what would the numbers have been?
**Not a validation.** One short window, a handful of trades at most: it can only describe, never show an edge. No claim of profitability will be made from it.

**Window.** Signals opened 2026-10-11 .. 2026-10-20 (IST dates, inclusive; the last 10 calendar days).
**Signals.** Exactly what `app.arena.run.ai_signals` returns (strategy AUTOSCALP, entry/SL/T1 as recorded, the AI's own exit replayed as EXIT_NOW). No filtering, no re-selection, nothing added or removed after seeing results.
**Settlement.** `app.arena.run.run` with the arena's current rules: captured option quotes, ask-in / bid-out, 20 s delay, SL + first target, session-end exit, real cost models, policy T1, capital Rs 50,000. SL-at-fill rule applies only from its effective instant (forward-only), so it is inactive for this window.
**Reported (all of them, whatever the sign).** n signals, n scorable vs unscorable (no captured quotes) with reasons, win rate, gross and net points and Rs, costs, per-day net, per-underlying split, the 95% bootstrap interval of per-trade net if n >= 10, else "n too small".
**Honesty rules.** Unscorable signals are listed, not dropped silently. If n < 30 the verdict is INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE regardless of sign. The result file is written unedited to `data/research/arena_backtest/`.
**Known limits to be stated with the result.** The AI engine almost stopped producing signals after the 2026-09-29 filter; captured quotes are snapshots about 1-3 minutes apart; no historical data can reproduce live fills; this is a replay of what the live engine did, not a fresh strategy search.
