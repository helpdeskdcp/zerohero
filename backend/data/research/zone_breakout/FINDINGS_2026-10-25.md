# Zone Breakout (strict consolidation) — owner's Angel One bot: verification, all-index back-test, calibration — findings 2026-10-25
Spec (committed 0c0a59f before the formal back-test; the 2-year NIFTY smoke run that preceded it is disclosed there): SPEC_2026-10-25.md. Code: app/zone_breakout/ (strategy, data, backtest, live, api), scripts/zone_breakout_{pull,backtest,calibrate}.py, tests/test_zone_breakout.py (5 tests). Original file kept unchanged in original/.

## 1. Verification of the uploaded bot
- It compiles and runs. In its own csv-backtest mode on 2 years of NIFTY 5m it produced 2,891 events (323 paper trades).
- PARITY: the re-implementation in app/zone_breakout reproduces ALL 2,891 events of the original (event type, timestamp, price) exactly on that file, and the entry/exit/stop/target events on a synthetic file in the unit test. The indicator flags are causal (a test rewrites the future and the past does not change).
- Defects / weaknesses found in the original (with the evidence):
  1. No session rule: positions stay open until stop or target; 8 % of the 323 paper trades were held overnight, up to 3 days (intraday indices should be flat by 15:15). Fixed in the intraday variants.
  2. The stop is the signal bar's own range: median risk 7.7 bp, 23 % of trades risk < 5 bp. A 1 bp round-trip cost is then 0.13 R on a median trade and > 0.25 R on 15 % of them. The file has no cost model, so its paper results look better than they are.
  3. Backtest mode prints events only: no R, win rate or expectancy. Added.
  4. Live candle builder: its epoch-based bucketing aligns 5- and 15-minute bars to the exchange clock but NOT 30- and 60-minute bars (tested with the original code: 30m bars start at :00/:30 instead of 09:15/09:45, 60m bars at 08:30/09:30 instead of 09:15/10:15), so a seed from SmartAPI history and the live bars would not line up for those intervals; the first live candle after a start is built from the ticks that arrive mid-candle (partial OHLC); a websocket reconnect creates a fresh builder and drops the forming candle.
  5. LTP-mode ticks carry no volume, so the volume filter can never be satisfied live (it defaults to off, correctly).
  6. It logs into Angel One on its own. In this system a second SmartAPI login has already knocked out the main session before (the OpenBull incident), so it was replaced by the primary Angel path (below).
  7. Entry at the signal-bar close is optimistic by construction; measured effect here is small (variant B vs A below).
  8. Pine v1.3 itself was not provided, so parity with the Pine script (as opposed to this Python port) cannot be tested.

## 2. Back-test, all six indices (variant A = realistic: next-bar open entry, flat 15:15, no signal after 15:00; cost in R = cost_bp / risk_bp)

Verdict (pre-registered rule): NO-GO at 5 and 15 minutes (net R is positive only in 2015-19 and its CI includes 0 there; negative in 2020-23 and 2024-26). The gross edge is real but small and shrinking (+0.16 R -> +0.08 R -> +0.08 R at 5m, win rate 39 % -> 36 % -> 37 % against a 33 % break-even), and the cost on stops this tight (median 9 bp) takes all of it. Every index is net negative in 2024-26 (NIFTY -0.12, BANKNIFTY -0.05, SENSEX -0.15, FINNIFTY -0.14, MIDCPNIFTY -0.12, BANKEX -0.05 R at 5m); the only positive cells are NIFTY 2015-19 (+0.08 / +0.15 R, CI includes 0) and SENSEX 15m (+0.04, n 156-178). Entry at the signal close (B) and the original's overnight mode (C) change nothing material (pooled P1 net +0.035 / +0.058, P2 -0.069 / -0.071).

## 3. Calibration (pre-registered: 72-config grid on 2015-19, one test on 2020-23)
Best in-sample config: RR 3.0, cons_bars 2, max_cons_atr 1.0, EMA filter off: P1 net +0.131 R (CI lower -0.006 even in sample, 72 trials). Out of sample: P2 (2020-23) net -0.055 R (gross +0.055, CI lower -0.191), P3 (seen) net -0.040 R. Verdict FAIL_P2: the calibrated config is not used; the default stays, and it is also NO-GO.
