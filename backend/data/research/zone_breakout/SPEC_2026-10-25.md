# Zone Breakout (Strict Consolidation) bot — verification, all-index back-test and calibration spec (pre-registered 2026-10-25, before the formal back-test)

## Disclosure
The owner's file (data/research/zone_breakout/original/zone_breakout_angelone_paper_bot.py, a Python port of a Pine v1.3 logic) was run once in its own csv-backtest mode on 2 years of NIFTY 5-minute bars (2024-01 .. 2026-05) BEFORE this spec: 323 paper trades, win 36.2 % (break-even at RR 2 is 33.3 %), gross +0.087 R per trade, net -0.068 R after a 1 bp cost; 8 % of positions were held overnight (up to 3 days); median risk 7.7 bp, 23 % of trades risk < 5 bp. To keep the confirmatory test independent of that slice, the confirmatory periods end in 2023; 2024-2026 is reported as "seen" (descriptive).

## Strategy (the bot's logic, unchanged)
Zone = previous 20 bars' high / low; ATR(14) Wilder; breakout = close beyond the zone by 0.05 ATR after a close inside; the breakout level is frozen; after `cons_bars` = 3 further completed bars that all hold beyond the level and a tight box (range of the prior 3 bars <= 1.5 ATR), a bullish / bearish ENGULFING or HARAMI candle (body >= 0.10 ATR) in the breakout direction with EMA(50) trend agreement (close above / below) triggers the trade; stop = signal bar low / high -/+ 0.1 ATR; target = RR 2.0 x risk; pending setup expires after 12 bars or when the close falls back through the level; stop wins a same-bar tie. Volume filter OFF (cash indices have no volume).

## Verification (before any performance number)
(1) causality: the signal flags at bar t do not change when later bars are changed; (2) PARITY: the re-implementation in app/zone_breakout reproduces the original script's PAPER_ENTRY / PAPER_EXIT events (timestamps and prices) exactly on the same CSV in the original's own mode (entry at the signal close, no session rule); (3) a list of defects found in the original (kept in FINDINGS).

## Test variants (all on the same signals; costs in R = cost_bp / risk_bp)
A (PRIMARY, realistic intraday): entry = OPEN of the bar after the signal bar (risk and target re-based on that open; the setup is skipped if the open is already beyond the stop); no signal on a bar starting at/after 15:00 IST; any open position is closed at the close of the bar that ends at/after 15:15 IST; pending-breakout state is reset when the session date changes. B: as A but entry at the signal-bar close (the bot's paper behaviour). C (ORIGINAL): entry at the signal close, positions may stay open overnight, no session rule (descriptive; shows what the file does as written).
Instruments / data (5-minute bars, front of the index): NIFTY and BANKNIFTY 2015-2026 (Kaggle, de-duplicated), SENSEX 2021-09..2026 (1-minute captures aggregated), FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY, BANKEX 2024-10..2026 (Upstox 1-minute historical aggregated). Time frames: 5 minutes (primary) and 15 minutes (aggregated from 5). Round-trip cost: NIFTY / BANKNIFTY 1.0 bp, SENSEX / BANKEX 1.5 bp, FINNIFTY / MIDCPNIFTY 2.0 bp (assumed).

## Periods
P1 = 2015-2019 (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY), P2 = 2020-2023 (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, SENSEX from 2021-09), P3 = 2024-2026 ("seen", all six indices, descriptive).

## Verdict (variant A; K = 2 time frames -> CI level 0.025 two-sided)
GO-SHADOW only if, for a time frame, in BOTH P1 and P2: pooled trades >= 300, mean net R > 0 with day-block bootstrap CI lower bound > 0, and NIFTY and BANKNIFTY each have mean net R > 0. Otherwise NO-GO (INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE if the pooled trade count is under 300). Also reported: win rate vs the 33.3 % break-even, profit factor, trades by risk bucket (< 5 bp, 5-10, > 10 bp), by year.

## Calibration (decided now, run ONLY if the default is NO-GO)
Grid on P1 (NIFTY + BANKNIFTY, 5 minutes, variant A): RR {1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 3.0} x cons_bars {2, 3, 5} x EMA filter {on, off} x max_cons_atr {1.0, 1.5, 2.5} = 72 configurations, selected by mean net R with >= 300 P1 trades. The single selected configuration is then evaluated ONCE on P2 with the same gates (trade threshold 150) and once on P3 (descriptive). The in-sample P1 value is reported with the trial count (72) and is not evidence; only P2 is. A config that fails P2 is not used.
