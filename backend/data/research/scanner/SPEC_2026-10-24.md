# ZeroHero in-house daily scanner — scan library + historical/forward validation spec (pre-registered 2026-10-24, before any scan is run on history)

## Universe and data (daily bars)
NIFTY (NSE index), BANKNIFTY, SENSEX (BSE index), NATURALGAS, CRUDEOIL, GOLDM (MCX front-month futures, volume-chained and ratio-adjusted at rolls). History available at spec time: NIFTY 2006-2026, BANKNIFTY / SENSEX / NATURALGAS / CRUDEOIL 2021-09..2026, GOLDM only the active contracts (about 5 months, thin early days dropped): GOLDM cannot be back-tested, it is scanned and forward-tracked only.
Scans are evaluated on COMPLETED daily bars. NSE instruments after 15:30 IST, MCX after 23:30 IST.

## Scan library (fixed now; sides fixed now; a scan may be added later but never edited after its first historical result)
Long: BRK20_UP (close > highest high of the previous 20 days), EMACROSS_UP (EMA20 crosses above EMA50), RSI_OS_UP (RSI14 crosses up through 30), TDB_LOW20 (three lower closes in a row and close within 0.5 ATR of the 20-day low), GAP_DN_BUY (open more than 0.5 ATR below the previous low), BBSQ_UP (Bollinger bandwidth at its 120-day low within the last 5 days and close above the upper band), BIGBAR_UP (range > 2 ATR and close in the top 25 %), PWH_BRK_UP, PMH_BRK_UP (close above previous week / month high).
Short: BRK20_DN, EMACROSS_DN, RSI_OB_DN (RSI14 crosses down through 70), TUB_HIGH20 (three higher closes and close within 0.5 ATR of the 20-day high), GAP_UP_SELL (open more than 0.5 ATR above the previous high), BBSQ_DN, BIGBAR_DN, PWL_BRK_DN, PML_BRK_DN.
K = 18 scans.

## Historical test (the back-test the data allows)
Signal on bar D; entry = open of D+1; exit = close of D+h for h in {1, 3, 5, 10} (h counts trading days, h=1 exits the same day D+1). Return in bps, side-adjusted. Overlapping signals: a new signal of the same scan on the same instrument within h days of the last counted one is skipped. Round-trip cost (assumed, not measured): NIFTY / BANKNIFTY / SENSEX 3 bp, GOLDM 5 bp, CRUDEOIL 8 bp, NATURALGAS 10 bp. Sensitivity 0.5x and 2x reported, not gated.
Pooled per scan across the instruments that have history; candidate horizons 3, 5, 10 (h=1 descriptive).

## Verdict per scan (per horizon, then scan-level)
INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE if the pooled count of counted signals < 100. Otherwise a horizon PASSES only if all hold: mean net return > 0; month-block bootstrap 95 % CI lower bound > 0 at the Bonferroni level 0.05 / K (K = 18); the sign is positive in BOTH halves of the sample (split by date); and it stays > 0 after dropping any single instrument. Scan label: GO-SHADOW if >= 2 of the 3 candidate horizons pass, else NO-GO ("single-horizon pass: not enough" if exactly one). Nothing is ever a trade gate. Paper / observation only.

## Forward tracking
Every daily run stores each scan hit with the signal close; the same horizons are measured forward from real data and reported separately from the historical result.

## Not claimed
Costs are assumptions. Index "volume" is mostly zero on the cash indices, so no volume scans are in the library. GOLDM and the 2021+ instruments have <= 5 years: a pass here is weak evidence; a fail is stronger evidence than a pass.

## Clarification (same day, written after the data audit and BEFORE any scan was run on history)
The stored MCX daily history (NATURALGAS, CRUDEOIL) is a raw per-contract series that is NOT adjusted at contract rolls (e.g. NATURALGAS 597.8 -> 342.8 = -43 % on 2026-01-28 is the January -> February contract, not a market move; 25 NG days moved > 12 %). Any breakout / gap / ATR scan on it would fire on roll artefacts, and per-contract MCX history before the active contracts is not available (Upstox expired-futures list is empty for MCX). Therefore:
1. The HISTORICAL test uses NIFTY (2006-2026), BANKNIFTY and SENSEX (2021-2026) only — cash indices, no rolls. Pooling and all gates are unchanged; K stays 18.
2. NATURALGAS, CRUDEOIL, GOLDM are scanned and forward-tracked only, on the Upstox active-contract daily series chained by volume and ratio-adjusted at rolls (about 120 trading days now, growing daily). The stored raw MCX base history is not used for them.
