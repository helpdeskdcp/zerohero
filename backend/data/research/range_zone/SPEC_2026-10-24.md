# Range & Zone signals — pre-registered spec (2026-10-24, before any result)
Owner's request: "find the range and the zone, then give the signal". ZeroHero already has swing-cluster support/resistance zones (app/sr_dynamic) but no range detector and no range/zone signal. This spec fixes one.

## Definitions (5-minute bars, causal; bar t is the last closed bar)
- ATR = Wilder ATR(14).
- Range (box) at t: the N = 24 bars t-24 .. t-1 (all in the same session, so t is at least the 25th bar) with (max high - min low) <= 3.0 ATR(t-1) AND each edge has been tested at least twice: >= 2 bars whose HIGH is within 0.25 ATR of the box high and >= 2 bars whose LOW is within 0.25 ATR of the box low. RH = box high, RL = box low, MID = (RH+RL)/2.
- Zone = the range edge band: upper zone [RH - 0.25 ATR, RH + 0.25 ATR], lower zone [RL - 0.25 ATR, RL + 0.25 ATR] (an edge tested twice or more is by construction a two-touch swing zone).
## Signals (4 variants, K = 4)
- BRK_UP: close(t) > RH and close(t-1) <= RH (first close outside, box valid at t-1) -> BUY.  BRK_DN: close(t) < RL and close(t-1) >= RL -> SELL.
- FADE_LONG: low(t) <= RL + 0.10 ATR and close(t) > RL and close(t) > open(t) (wick into the lower zone, closes back inside, bullish bar) -> BUY.  FADE_SHORT: high(t) >= RH - 0.10 ATR and close(t) < RH and close(t) < open(t) -> SELL.
Entry = open of bar t+1.
## Exits evaluated (each a separate test)
(a) fixed horizons h in {3, 6, 12} bars to the close of bar t+h, same session; (b) STRUCTURE exit: stop and target set at the signal bar close, first touch on later bars decides, stop wins a same-bar tie, time stop after 24 bars or the session end at that close. BRK_*: stop = opposite side of the entry zone (BRK_UP: RH - 0.25 ATR; BRK_DN: RL + 0.25 ATR), target = entry +/- the range height (RH - RL). FADE_*: stop = beyond the zone (RL - 0.25 ATR / RH + 0.25 ATR), target = MID.
A signal within h bars (STRUCTURE: 24 bars) of the last counted one of the same variant is skipped.
## Data, periods, cost
NIFTY and BANKNIFTY 5-minute index bars 2015-2026 (Kaggle, de-duplicated); P1 = 2015-2020, P2 = 2021-2026; round-trip cost 1.0 bp (0.5 / 2 reported only).
## Gates for GO-SHADOW (per variant; ALL)
NIFTY AND BANKNIFTY, P1 AND P2, for at least 2 of the 4 exit evaluations (h3, h6, h12, STRUCTURE): n >= 300 counted signals, mean net return > 0, day-block bootstrap CI lower bound > 0 at the Bonferroni level 0.05 / 8. n < 300 in any cell -> INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE; enough data but a gate fails -> NO-GO. Baselines (descriptive): the same signal without the edge-tested-twice condition, and the unconditional same-side drift.
