# Pre-registered spec: option-level expiry-day premise (NIFTY), written BEFORE any outcome was computed

**Why this exists.** The index-level premise test (`SPEC_2026-10-21.md`) failed for NIFTY (late expiry-day index range 0.95x of normal). An expiring option is convex, so its premium can still move a lot while the index range does not. Premium %-range is NOT a valid yardstick (a tiny premium inflates any %-range and the costs scale the same way: the convexity artefact found earlier), so the premise is tested directly net of costs.
**Hypothesis H-OPT-EXP.** A long ATM straddle (1 CE + 1 PE of the expiring contract) bought at 14:30 IST on NIFTY option-expiry day and sold at 15:15 earns a positive net result after spread and statutory costs. A direction-free straddle isolates "does the expansion pay more than theta + friction"; if even this does not pay, a directional single long option cannot rely on volatility alone.
**Data.** `data/historical/upstox/upstox_research.db` `normalized_option_bars` (NIFTY, 5-minute bars, IST timestamps), all expiry dates present in it (expected ~60, 2025-08-28 .. 2026-10-13). One row per expiry day. No expiry is added or removed after seeing results.
**ATM rule (information known before entry).** On day E, the strike whose CE close and PE close of the 14:25 bar (bar closing at 14:30) have the smallest absolute difference (ties: lower strike). Requires CE and PE rows at the 14:25, 14:30 and 15:10 bars; otherwise the day is skipped and listed.
**Trade.** Entry = open of the 14:30 bar of each leg, plus a half-spread penalty s * price; exit = close of the 15:10 bar (= 15:15) of each leg, minus s * price. **s = 1.5% per side (primary).** Statutory costs: the arena cost model (`app.arena.settle.cost_points`) per leg with the current NIFTY lot size. Net per straddle in Rs = (sum of leg exits - sum of leg entries) * lot - costs of both legs. No stop, no target, no re-entry.
**Control (descriptive only).** The same rule applied to the previous trading day's same contract (DTE 1). Not part of the gate.
**Gate (premise holds only if ALL).** (1) n_used >= 30; (2) mean net per straddle > 0; (3) the 97.5% two-sided bootstrap interval of the mean net (5000 resamples, seed 7; 97.5% leaves room for a second index, SENSEX) has a lower bound > 0; (4) mean net > 0 in BOTH halves of the sample (split at the median date); (5) the mean net is still > 0 with s = 1.0%. Reported also: s = 0 (gross), s = 2.0%, median, win rate, per-regime split (Thursday-expiry era before 2025-09-01 vs Tuesday-expiry era).
**Not claimed.** A pass is a premise only: no directional edge, no live fills (5-minute bars, assumed spread), no capacity. A fail drops NIFTY expiry-day long-option buying. Result written unedited to `data/research/index_event/`.
