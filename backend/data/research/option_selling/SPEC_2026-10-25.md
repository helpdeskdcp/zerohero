# Defined-risk weekly option selling (volatility risk premium) on NIFTY and SENSEX — pre-registered spec (2026-10-25, before any structure is priced)

## Why
Every price-direction rule tested so far fails after costs. Selling index option premium is a different source of return: implied volatility has historically exceeded realised volatility (the variance risk premium), paid for by occasional large losses. Earlier work here (NIFTY weekly iron condor, intraday, 2025) was NO-GO but used MODEL prices (Black-Scholes approximation). We now hold the real 1-minute prices: 108 NIFTY and 107 SENSEX weekly expiries (2024-10 .. 2026-10), the last 5 sessions of each, strikes ATM +/-6 steps (data/historical/option_flow/*.db: close, volume, open interest). No bid / ask: fills are modelled with an explicit slippage.

## Structure (fixed now; defined risk only — no naked short options)
SHORT IRON CONDOR, 1 lot, built at the entry time from the real prices: ST = ATM straddle (call + put close at the strike nearest to the index). d = max(2 steps, round(m x ST / step) x step) with m = 1.0 (primary). Short call at ATM + d, short put at ATM - d, long wings 2 steps further out (NIFTY 100 points, SENSEX 200 points beyond the shorts). Credit = sum of the short legs - sum of the wings. A trade is skipped (and counted) if any of the four strikes has no price within the 5 minutes before the decision time or if the net credit after slippage is not positive. Steps: NIFTY 50, SENSEX 100. Current lot sizes (NIFTY 65, SENSEX 20) so rupee P&L is comparable across the sample.
Pricing: the decision at HH:MM:00 uses the close of the 1-minute bar that started one minute earlier (what was known then); entry and exit prices are those closes plus slippage.

## Variants (K = 3 per index -> 6 gated cells; CI level 0.05 / 6 = 0.0083 two-sided)
V0 0DTE: expiry day, enter 09:30, exit 15:15 (no overnight). V1 1DTE intraday: the session before expiry, enter 09:30, exit 15:15. V2 1DTE overnight: enter 15:00 on the session before expiry, exit 15:15 on expiry day (carries one overnight). If the weekly series has fewer sessions (holiday weeks) the variant uses the sessions that exist.
Descriptive only (not gated): m in {0.75, 1.25}; a 50 %-of-credit profit target and a stop at a loss equal to the credit (checked every minute) on V0 / V1.

## Costs (per trade, rupees; rates from app/institutional_edge/costs.py RegulatoryRateCard, verified 2026-09-28)
Brokerage Rs 20 per order, 8 orders (4 legs in, 4 out) = Rs 160; exchange transaction 0.0355299 % of premium value, both sides, all legs; STT on the SELL side only (entry: the short legs; exit: the long wings sold back): 0.10 % of premium value before 2026-04-01 and 0.15 % from 2026-04-01; stamp duty 0.003 % on BUY side value; SEBI 0.0001 % of all premium value; GST 18 % of (brokerage + exchange + SEBI). Slippage assumed: NIFTY 0.5 point per leg per side, SENSEX 2.0 points per leg per side (8 leg-sides per trade).

## Verdict per cell (variant x index): GO-SHADOW only if ALL hold
n >= 80 trades; mean net P&L per trade > 0; month-block bootstrap 95 %-style CI (level 0.05 / 6) lower bound of the mean > 0; the mean is positive in both halves of the sample (by date). Otherwise NO-GO (INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE if n < 80). Reported with every verdict: win rate, worst single trade, 5 % expected shortfall, maximum drawdown, the maximum loss possible by construction (width x lot - credit), and the skip count. A positive mean with a worst trade larger than 10 mean-winning trades is flagged as a tail-risk profile, not a pass by itself.

## Not claimed
1-minute closes are not fills; real spreads on the wings can be wider than assumed; the sample is two years and about 100 expiries per index (one regime); a pass would license an observation-only live feed, never an order.
