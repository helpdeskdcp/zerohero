# Operator hypotheses: index-vs-constituent divergence and expiry pinning — pre-registered spec (2026-10-25, before any statistic is computed)
Owner's claims to test: (A) the index is moved by money placed in heavyweight stocks, so on expiry days the index should be LESS explained by its constituents than on other days; (B) on expiry days price is steered toward a strike (the "max pain" strike) so that most OTM options die. Both are tests of a mechanism, not of a trading rule; a positive finding would only motivate a separate pre-registered trading test.

## Test A — is the index explained by its constituents less on expiry days?
Data: daily closes of the 53-stock large-cap list (app/scanner/stocks.py) and the NIFTY 5-minute index file collapsed to daily closes, 2024-10-03 .. 2026-10-19 (the period for which the expiry calendar is known from the option database: 107 NIFTY weekly expiries; "expiry day" = last session of each expiry). Model: daily NIFTY log-return on the 53 stock log-returns, ridge regression (alpha fixed 1e-4 on standardised returns), fitted on NON-expiry days of the 2024-10 .. 2025-09 year, evaluated out-of-sample on 2025-10 .. 2026-10 (that is the pre-registered split). Statistic: residual standard deviation on expiry days divided by residual standard deviation on non-expiry days, in the out-of-sample year; p-value by permutation of the expiry labels (10,000). Pre-registered claim "expiry days less explained" = ratio > 1 with permutation p < 0.025 (two tests, Bonferroni). The reverse (ratio < 1) is reported, not claimed.

## Test B — does the expiry-day price move toward the max-pain strike?
Data: NIFTY weekly option database (open interest and 1-minute closes), 107 expiry days. Max-pain strike K* = argmin over strikes in the stored band of total writer payout, computed from open interest of the PREVIOUS session's last bar (known before the expiry day opens). Index proxy at time t = put-call-parity forward at the nearest-parity strike (K + C - P). Statistic: pull = (|F(09:30) - K*| - |F(15:15) - K*|) / |F(09:30) - K*| (positive = moved toward K*). Placebo: the same statistic with K* replaced by the max-pain strike of a different, randomly chosen expiry shifted to the same price level (offset = F(09:30) - K*_own preserved), 10,000 permutations. Pre-registered claim "pinning" = median pull > 0 AND observed mean pull above the placebo 97.5th percentile. Also reported: share of expiry days that close within 25 points of K*, versus the placebo share.
Honest limits: the stored strike band is about +/- 12 steps, so max pain is computed on the band only; OI is exchange-reported one-day-lagged style data; 107 events; a hit would not say who steers (hedging flow of option writers ("pin risk") produces pinning without any manipulation).
Both tests: no parameter is tuned after seeing a result.
