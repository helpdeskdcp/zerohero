# Operator hypotheses — FINDINGS 2026-10-25 (spec SPEC_2026-10-25.md, commit 371c96d; script scripts/operator_tests.py; results.json). Paper/research only.

## Test A — is the index explained by its constituents less on expiry days?  NOT SUPPORTED
Ridge of NIFTY daily returns on 53 stocks, fitted on non-expiry days 2024-10..2025-09, evaluated 2025-10..2026-10 (262 days, 55 expiry days).
R² out-of-sample: 97.0 % on normal days, 96.5 % on expiry days. Residual-sd ratio expiry/normal = 1.09, permutation p = 0.22 (needed > 1 with p < 0.025). So the index is explained almost fully by its constituents on expiry days as on every other day; no sign that it is moved by something outside the stocks.

## Test B — does price move toward the max-pain strike on expiry day?  NOT SUPPORTED (opposite direction)
106 NIFTY expiry days. Max pain from the previous session's OI (stored band), price proxy = put-call-parity forward.
Median pull -0.74 (positive would be "toward"); mean absolute distance to max pain grew from 102 pts (09:30) to 162 pts (15:15). Share closing within 25 pts of max pain: 5.7 % vs 12.1 % under the placebo (p for "more than placebo" = 0.99). Pinning claim: false. (Exploratory only, not pre-registered: close is, if anything, further from max pain than chance; with 106 events and a band-limited max pain I do not claim a reverse effect.)

## Reading
The expiry-day OTM collapse (70-96 % end at <= Rs 0.5) is explained by theta/gamma on a day when the index usually moves less than the strike distance, not by steering to a strike, and the index is not moving independently of its stocks. This does not disprove that any individual actor ever moves a price; it shows these two mechanisms leave no measurable footprint in 2 years of NIFTY data, and gives no tradable signal. A different test of "stock money moves the index" would need intraday constituent data (not held).
