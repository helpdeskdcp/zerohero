# Chartink scanner alerts — forward-validation spec (pre-registered 2026-10-24, before any alert exists)

## What is measured
Every Chartink alert received on the webhook lists NSE symbols that matched a scan. For each (alert, symbol) the tracker records a reference price (first LTP at/after receipt, must be within 120 s, else the pair is flagged LATE and excluded) and then LTP at +5, +15, +30, +60 minutes and at 15:25 IST (EOD), plus NIFTY at the same instants. Pairs whose horizon falls after 15:25 are not extended past the close.

## Per scan
- Side: LONG by default; a scan whose logic is bearish is set to SHORT in data/chartink/scans.json BEFORE its first alert is evaluated (the side may not be changed after looking at results).
- Return r_h = side * (P_h / P_0 - 1) in bps. Market-adjusted excess e_h = r_h - side * (N_h / N_0 - 1) in bps (N = NIFTY).
- Cost: equity intraday round trip 8 bps assumed (brokerage + STT + exchange + stamp + slippage on liquid stocks); sensitivity 5 / 10 / 15 bps reported, not gated.
- Unit of resampling: the alert-day (all symbols of all alerts of one scan on one day are one block), because symbols in one alert are strongly correlated.

## Verdict per scan and horizon (30 and 60 minutes and EOD are the candidate horizons; 5 / 15 min are descriptive)
- INSUFFICIENT_SAMPLE until the scan has >= 30 alert-days AND >= 300 valid (alert, symbol) pairs.
- GO-SHADOW (worth keeping as an observation feed) only if, at the candidate horizon, mean e_h - 8 bps > 0 with alert-day block-bootstrap 95 % CI lower bound > 0 AND the same sign in both halves of the sample (first and second half by date). Nothing here is ever a trade gate; paper only.
- Else NO-GO for that scan. A scan with fewer than 30 alert-days is never declared good or bad.
- Multiple scans: every scan counts as a trial; with K scans the CI level is Bonferroni-adjusted to 1 - 0.05/K.

## Data caveats stated up front
Chartink payload times are minute-level and have no date; the receive time at our server is authoritative. LTP is Angel One REST (read-only). Survivorship / corporate actions: intraday only, so none. No orders are placed.

## Clarification (same day, written before any alert exists; no result has been seen)
A scan is labelled GO-SHADOW only if at least 2 of the 3 candidate horizons (30 min, 60 min, EOD) pass individually (each with the K-scan adjusted CI). A single passing horizon is shown as "single-horizon pass: not enough" to prevent horizon picking. The 5 and 15 minute rows stay descriptive.
