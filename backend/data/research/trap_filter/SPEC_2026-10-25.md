# Trap / SL-hunt filter on the Zone Breakout signal — pre-registered spec (2026-10-25, before any feature is computed)
Question (owner's): can a filter that recognises "operator trap / stop-hunt" breakouts remove enough losers to make the signal profitable after costs?
Population: every trade of the Zone Breakout bot, variant A (5-minute, next-bar-open entry, flat 15:15, no signal after 15:00, default Config), all six indices, full history (about 6,000+ trades). Label: win = net_R > 0 (R minus cost_bp/risk_bp). Unfiltered baseline: +0.16/+0.08/+0.08 gross R, net about -0.03 to -0.1 R.
Features: computed ONLY from bars up to and including the signal bar (a test rewrites future bars and asserts features are unchanged). No volume (index volume is a placeholder). Trap / stop-hunt proxies:
 1 adverse wick of the signal bar / its range (wick on the side AGAINST the trade); 2 body/range; 3 close location in range (trade-direction signed); 4 range/ATR; 5 zone height/ATR; 6 distance of entry from zone edge in ATR;
 7 sweep flag: signal bar took out the previous 12-bar extreme (in trade direction) and still closed back inside; 8 prior failed breakouts in the same session on the same side (trap history); 9 distance of entry to previous-day high/low in ATR (stop-cluster proxy) and whether the entry is on the far side of it;
 10 position in the day range so far; 11 distance to the day's VWAP-like running mean in ATR (signed); 12 minutes since open; 13 overnight gap %; 14 prior 12-bar move in ATR signed by trade side; 15 ATR percentile (rolling 100 bars); 16 EMA50 slope in ATR; 17 side; 18 risk_bp; 19 weekday.
Models (K=2, CI level 0.05/2 = 0.025): L2 logistic regression (standardised) and shallow HistGradientBoosting (depth 3, 100 iterations, min leaf 50, L2). No tuning on the test years; hyper-parameters fixed here.
Protocol: expanding-window walk-forward, pooled instruments: train on all trades with entry year < Y, test on year Y, for Y = 2019 ... 2026 (first train block 2015-2018). A test-year trade is TAKEN if predicted P(win) >= 0.42 (fixed: break-even win rate at RR 2 is 1/3, plus about 8 points margin; not tuned). Metrics on the pooled out-of-sample taken trades.
Gate (all must hold, per model; otherwise NO-GO): OOS AUC >= 0.55; taken n >= 300; mean net R of taken trades > 0; session-block bootstrap CI (level 0.025) lower bound > 0; taken mean net R exceeds the unfiltered OOS mean by >= 0.10 R; positive in >= 5 of the 8 test years; positive in 2025-26 combined.
Also reported (not gated): coverage (share taken), per-instrument split, feature importances, permutation control (train labels shuffled -> same pipeline -> AUC and taken net R, 20 repeats) to show the noise floor.
Honest limits stated now: the proxies see only price, not stops or order flow; the L2 / aggressor data that could see a hunt directly exists only for the last days (retention 7-21 days), far too short for this test; a pass licenses observation-only forward paper tracking of the filtered signal, never orders.
