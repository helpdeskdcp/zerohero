# Large-cap short-term reversal, market-neutral — pre-registered spec (2026-10-25, before any ranking is computed)
Universe: the 53-stock large-cap list in app/scanner/stocks.py, daily bars 2019-01 .. 2026-10 (Upstox cache in scanner.db). SURVIVORSHIP: the list is today's constituents; stocks that fell and were later dropped are absent, which FLATTERS a buy-the-losers rule. A pass is therefore suspect; a fail is strong.
Signal (computed on the close of day t, no later data): trailing L-day close-to-close return. Rank the stocks that day; LONG the lowest 20 % (10 stocks), SHORT the highest 20 % (10), equal weight, dollar-neutral. Enter at the close of day t+1 (one-day delay, no look-ahead), exit at the close of t+1+H. Non-overlapping: new portfolio every H days.
Cells (K = 2, CI level 0.05/2 = 0.025): (L=5,H=5) weekly reversal; (L=21,H=21) monthly reversal.
Net return per rebalance = mean(long returns) - mean(short returns) - 2 x c, c = round-trip cost per leg of 10 bps (futures-style: brokerage+STT+exchange ~3 bps + 3 bps slippage per side, applied to both baskets at entry and exit). Sensitivity (not gated): c = 20 bps (cash delivery style, STT 0.1 % each side).
Gate (all must hold): n rebalances >= 100 (L=5) / >= 40 (L=21); mean net > 0; block-bootstrap CI (level 0.025, 4-rebalance blocks) lower bound > 0; positive in both halves; positive in at least 5 of the 7 calendar years; still > 0 at c = 20 bps is reported, not gated. Otherwise NO-GO. Also reported: long-only leg vs the equal-weight basket (to show whether any result is just beta/universe drift), hit rate, worst rebalance, max drawdown.
A pass would license observation-only forward tracking, never orders. Short cash-segment positions cannot be held overnight; any real implementation would need single-stock futures.
