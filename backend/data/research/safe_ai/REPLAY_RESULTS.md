# Safe AI — 12-month NIFTY backtest (Upstox 1-minute index + expired weekly option candles)

**Scope:** NIFTY only (SENSEX options are not available historically; BANKNIFTY is pulled separately), days 0, 1 and 2 before the weekly expiry, 60 weekly expiries 2025-09-09 → 2026-10-27, 1 lot, safety stack of the live runner (<= 2 trades a day, one loss ends the day, 30-minute cool-down, window 09:35-14:30, 13:30 on the expiry day, ATM within one strike, premium >= Rs 30). Stop 25 % of the premium, first target +20 %, checked on each minute's low/high (stop first if one bar touches both). No bid/ask in the data: slippage = half-spread each way. Costs = the arena's full charge model. Pre-registered grid (3 thrusts x with/without the OI filter, 1 sweep family), DEV = to 31-Mar-2026, HOLD-OUT = from 1-Apr-2026.

**Reading it honestly**
* No cell is statistically different from zero: every 95 % interval of the net per trade contains 0.
* The default rule (U-turn, thrust 0.50 %) makes about +Rs 20/trade at a 0.5 % half-spread (+Rs 64 at 0.25 %, -Rs 10 at 1 %) — break-even territory, win rate ~60 %. The hold-out (+Rs 84/trade, n=31) is better than the development period (-Rs 36/trade, n=36): that is not a stable edge, it is noise around zero.
* A looser thrust (0.35 %) is negative in the development period; a stricter one (0.70 %) is positive in the hold-out (n=15) and negative in development: sample far too small.
* The OI filter and the sweep family look a little better in places; with 8 cells x 3 spreads inspected, the best looking ones are partly luck (winner's curse). The OI filter was added after seeing partial results, so it is NOT used live.
* Worst single loss Rs 3.9k (8 % of the Rs 50,000 notional); worst drawdown over a year Rs 6-8k at 1 lot. That is the "safe" part: small, bounded, near-zero expected loss — not a profit claim.
