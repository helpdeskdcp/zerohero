# India-VIX "trap check" filter on the Winners Scalper signals — pre-registered spec, 2026-10-25, before any filter is applied
Owner's request: add the Pine script's own trap check - skip the entry when it fires. Definition copied from the script (MODULE 2): risk_trend_up = VIX close > VIX close 3 chart-bars earlier (chart timeframe 15m; the series is the VIX close aligned to the chart bars by timestamp and shifted by 3 bars, across session boundaries exactly as request.security would give it); is_fake_buy_risk = risk_trend_up and a BUY signal; is_fake_sell_risk = (not risk_trend_up) and a SELL signal (a flat VIX counts as "not up"). A signal flagged by either is a TRAP and is skipped (no entry); VIX is read at the close of the signal bar (known at entry).
Data: India VIX 15-minute candles from Upstox (2022-01-03 .. 2026-10-23; earlier history is not available), data/historical/vix/india_vix_15m.csv.
Signals and exits: exactly the pre-registered X2 / 15m rules of SPEC_2026-10-25.md; no parameter changed.
## Test 1 - index level (K = 2 tests in total, level 0.05/2 = 0.025)
Population: the six indices' X2/15m trades with entry in 2022-01 .. 2026-10 (VIX available). Compare KEPT (not a trap) with ALL. Gate (all): kept n >= 300; mean net R of kept exceeds that of ALL by >= 0.05 R; kept net R > 0 with session-block bootstrap CI lower bound (level 0.025) > 0; kept positive in >= 3 of the 5 calendar years 2022-2026; and permutation p < 0.025 (10,000 random removals of the same number of trades from ALL: how often does a random subset of the same size do as well as KEPT).
## Test 2 - option premiums (same gate in rupees)
Population: the 2024-10 .. 2026-10 NIFTY + SENSEX option-premium trades of SPEC_OPTIONS_2026-10-25.md (all days, options_trades_*.csv) pooled. Gate (all): kept n >= 150; mean net Rs/trade of kept > 0; day-block bootstrap CI lower bound (level 0.025) > 0; kept mean exceeds ALL by >= Rs 30; permutation p < 0.025.
Reported, not gated: share of signals flagged, mean of the removed trades, results for lookbacks 1 and 5 bars, and for the inverse filter (skipping the non-traps).
Not claimed: a pass would be a filter on a signal whose option-level mean is about zero; it would be observation-only material, never orders.
