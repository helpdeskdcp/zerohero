Source: the Pine v6 indicator pasted by the owner on 2026-10-25 (base: "Winners_Scalper_Pro v4.5 by sabritonyali", India port). Signal definition used = the script's own alertcondition "Buy Signal (All)" = initialBuy or lateStrongBuy, "Sell Signal (All)" = initialSell or lateStrongSell.
## What is ported (only the signal path; nothing else affects a signal)
ATR(6) (Wilder/rma), DMI/ADX(16,16), SMA(34) trend filter, the ATR trailing stops (longStop/shortStop, mult 2.1, length 6) and the `dir` state, `barssince`-based isTrendStable (minBars 0; note Pine's barssince is na until the first flip so no signal can fire before the first direction change), hasStrongFired logic, isCondStrong (adx > 14 and (close > MA or close > longStop x 1.0005)). TP levels = signal close +/- 1.5 / 3.0 / 4.0 x ATR. NOT used by any signal and therefore not ported: volume modules (volume proxy needs futures; spot indices have none), India VIX risk gauge, SM zones, dashboards, exhaustion, key levels. The chart warnings the owner saw (INDIAVIX, futures volume unavailable in the chart) therefore do not change any signal.

## Back-test (K = 4 cells, CI level 0.05/4 = 0.0125)
Data: the six indices' 5-minute bars used for zone_breakout (NIFTY, BANKNIFTY 2015-; SENSEX 2021-9-; FINNIFTY, MIDCPNIFTY, BANKEX 2024-10-), 15-minute built from 5-minute. Entry at the OPEN of the bar after the signal bar (the alert fires at the bar close), no signal after 15:00, flat at 15:15. Stop = the stop line of the signal bar (longStop for BUY, shortStop for SELL). R = P&L / |entry - stop|; net_R = R - cost_bp / risk_bp (same per-index costs as zone_breakout: 1 / 1 / 1.5 / 2 / 2 / 1.5 bp).
Exit variants: X1 = stop or TP1 (entry-bar-close based, 1.5 ATR) or 15:15; X2 = trailing: exit at the open after the direction flips (or the stop line is hit intrabar) or 15:15, no target. Cells: X1/X2 x 5m/15m, all six indices pooled.
Gate (all): n >= 300; mean net_R > 0; session-bootstrap CI lower bound (level 0.0125) > 0; net_R > 0 in at least 2 of the 3 periods P1 2015-19, P2 2020-23, P3 2024-26 including P3. Otherwise NO-GO. Reported, not gated: per-index split, strong-only vs all signals, win rate, TP1 hit rate.
