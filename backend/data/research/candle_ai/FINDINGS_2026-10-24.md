## What is real
- There IS a small, statistically significant next-candle signal: AUC 0.52-0.54, CI above 0.50 in 3 of 4 cells, positive in every test year 2018-2026. But it is almost all short-term mean reversion: a 3-input logistic baseline (last two bar returns + time of day) already gets 0.516-0.532; patterns + Fibonacci/previous D-W-M levels add only +0.004..+0.007 AUC (G4 requires 0.005 in every cell; it fails 2 of 4).
- It is decaying: NIFTY AUC by test year 0.531, 0.541, 0.530, 0.524, 0.516, 0.524, 0.522, 0.520, 0.512; BANKNIFTY 0.541 ... 0.522.
- It is too small to trade: the most confident 10% of bars hit 54-57%, but the gross move is only +0.2..+0.7 bp per 5-minute candle. Net of a 1 bp round trip it is negative everywhere; at 0.5 bp it is slightly positive in 3 of 4 cells and still negative in NIFTY P2. Option premiums, theta, spread and fills are NOT modelled and are far worse than 1 bp (earlier option-level work in this repo: costs destroyed every index-level edge).
- Calibration (G1): reliability error is excellent (0.004-0.008) but the slope is 0.63-0.72 (limit 0.8-1.2): the isotonic map fitted on 20% of the train window is over-dispersed on the test years (probabilities too extreme). A shrinkage/Platt re-calibration would probably fix the slope; it would not touch the edge size, so it was NOT done post hoc (would be a dated addendum if ever wanted).

## "Which candle, in which condition" (discovery 2015-2019, BH-FDR 0.10, replicated 2020+ at p<0.05, same sign)
Only 5 cells survive out of the whole pattern x level x trend grid, all three-bar "continuation" patterns that MEAN-REVERT next candle:
- three black crows near/at a support level (prev-day low touch, or Fib pivot S-side) in no/down trend -> next candle UP +6.8..+7.1 points above base (n 246-373 in 2020+); gross +0.9 bp, net -0.1 bp.
- three white soldiers near a Fibonacci pivot (either side) in a flat trend -> next candle DOWN 4.5-4.8 points below base (n 535-540); gross +0.2..+0.4 bp, net -0.6..-0.8 bp.
No single-bar pattern (doji, hammer, engulfing, pin, harami, ...), no previous week / month level, no daily-pivot R/S zone survived replication. Nothing reaches the cost.

## Volume candles (descriptive, ~80 sessions, no GO possible)
Price-only vs +volume AUC (test = last 30% of sessions): NIFTY fut 0.562 -> 0.571, BANKNIFTY 0.536 -> 0.541, NATURALGAS 0.514 -> 0.523, CRUDEOIL 0.502 -> 0.498. Volume adds +0.005..+0.009 AUC in 3 of 4, always inside the CI. The 80-session mean reversion is stronger than the 11-year average (close-location alone AUC 0.42 on NIFTY futures) but that is one short period. Index data has no volume, so the volume question cannot be tested on the long history at all.

## Decision
Not wired into autoscalp. Reason: fails the pre-registered gates; the only robust part (short-term mean reversion) is below cost and decaying. The engine and features stay in app/candle_ai/ as research tooling. If the owner still wants forward evidence, the cheap option is an observation-only shadow log (default OFF) — no decisions change.
